Coverage and limits

Current figures reflect the 2026 tax year. Historical lines are annual statutory-rate snapshots for 2021–2026. Portugal means mainland Portugal; Netherlands personal income uses Box 1 rates for people below the AOW state-pension age. Rates are marginal/statutory reference points, not a forecast of anyone’s final bill.

How to compare these numbers

  • Portugal’s 48% IRS top bracket excludes the separate solidarity surcharge; that surcharge can add 2.5 or 5 percentage points for higher taxable incomes. Personal deductions and credits are not included.
  • The Dutch first Box 1 band includes national-insurance contributions for most working-age residents; the Portuguese IRS rate shown is income tax only. That makes entry-band rates directionally useful, not perfectly like-for-like.
  • Portugal’s 15% corporate rate applies only to qualifying SMEs / Small Mid-Caps on their first €50,000 of taxable profit. Dutch corporate rates are 19% up to €200,000 and 25.8% above. Local additions, reliefs and special regimes are excluded.
  • Reduced and zero VAT rates apply only to legally eligible goods/services. The 0% Dutch rate is not a general domestic shopping rate. Regional Portuguese rates can differ.
